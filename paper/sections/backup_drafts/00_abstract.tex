\begin{abstract}
Diffusion-based exemplar style transfer aims to synthesize artistic textures while faithfully preserving the semantic geometry of target objects. However, existing training-free approaches face a persistent dilemma: aggressive stylization causes catastrophic content collapse and semantic leakage from style references, whereas conservative modulation results in severe under-stylization and textural dilution. In this paper, we propose \textbf{\method}, a training-free style transfer framework that establishes an optimal Pareto frontier without network fine-tuning or fragile inversion. \method integrates three complementary mechanisms: (1) \textbf{Blended Style Tokens} with energy-compensated orthogonal cross-attention to transfer rich, heavy artistic textures while suppressing object leakage; (2) \textbf{Score-Orthogonal DINO Guidance}, which projects style gradients onto the null space of the diffusion score vector to steer the sampling trajectory strictly along the data manifold; and (3) an \textbf{Early-Step AdaIN Latent Pushforward} on predicted clean latents to rapidly ground chromatic statistics before fine-grained texture synthesis begins. Theoretical proofs confirm that our orthogonal projection prevents out-of-distribution drift and semantic corruption. Extensive quantitative and qualitative evaluations on the 225-pair SOICT-Data benchmark demonstrate that \method achieves superior Pareto optimality over six state-of-the-art baselines, delivering a $+25.9\%$ increase in CSD style similarity, $+24.8\%$ higher DINO style alignment, and a $17.4\%$ reduction in LPIPS perceptual distortion over its foundational backbone.

\end{abstract}
